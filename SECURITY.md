# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability or Security Incident

Report suspected vulnerabilities, active exploitation, or security incidents affecting Json.NET Schema privately to [contact@newtonsoft.com](mailto:contact@newtonsoft.com).

Include, where possible:

- The affected package version.
- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Reproduction steps or a proof of concept.
- Whether exploitation has been observed or is suspected.
- Any known mitigations.

Do not disclose the issue publicly until a fix or mitigation is available and coordinated disclosure has been agreed. Do not include secrets, personal data, or other sensitive information that is not necessary to investigate the report.

## Response Process

Newtonsoft will promptly assess reports, coordinate remediation and disclosure, and inform affected users when appropriate. When a report concerns an actively exploited vulnerability or a severe incident affecting the security of Json.NET Schema, the maintainers will use the reporting process required by Article 14 of the EU Cyber Resilience Act, including:

- An early warning through the ENISA Single Reporting Platform within 24 hours of becoming aware.
- A full notification within 72 hours of becoming aware.
- A final report within the applicable statutory deadline.

Security fixes and advisories will be published through the project's normal release and GitHub advisory channels.
